FAQ

Common questions

How much does a project cost?

It depends on scope. Small business websites start from a fixed price; larger products are estimated after the discovery call. You always get a written estimate before any work starts.

Can you take over a project another developer started?

Yes, that's one of my core services. I start with a short code audit, tell you honestly what state it's in, and propose a plan to stabilize and continue it.

Do you work with stacks other than Next.js and Adonis?

Yes. For support and optimization work I'm comfortable with most JavaScript/TypeScript and Node.js stacks, plus common SQL databases. For new builds I recommend my core stack because it's fast to develop and easy to maintain.

What happens after launch?

You can choose a monthly support plan (fixes, updates, small features) or contact me as needed. Either way, you own everything and get documentation for it.
